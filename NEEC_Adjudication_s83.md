## 2. The rule (decision 83.4; the same for every entry)

1. **The protocol governs.** A verdict follows the clause statuses (2.3(a)); 1.0 needs every clause shown cleared on an
   estimate; 0.0 needs one of protocol 2.1's conditions for the criterion as a whole, stated, and never comes with a clause
   cleared (2.3(f), decision 53.2); an anchor band describes and does not govern (2.3(g)).
2. **The sources, in order:** the unit's pack (what the reader was given), the sources NEEC's record cites, and for a
   closed-book unit the record's sources and the reader's named sources, checked where this session could check them (the
   web was reachable; OECD's site refused the container, as before). Jev's clause choices are a check on what the pack shows,
   never a verdict.
3. **Silence is not clearance, on either side.** A NEEC clearance that rests only on the published rationale's words ("as
   audited") does not stand against a reading that finds no source for the clause; a reader clearance that rests on a plan, a
   claim or a reputation where the threshold asks for a figure, a record or precedent evidence does not stand either.
4. **One reading per class** (2.3(e)). Where the reader reads one entry differently from a like entry and the sources do not
   distinguish them, the like entries' common reading governs; where NEEC does, its earlier sessions' class readings govern
   (Session 53 on C1.2a, 54 on C1.3, 71 on C4.5, 73 on C4.2 and C4.4, 74 on C3.1, 76 and 77 on C2.6, C3.6 and C4.6).
5. **Outcome.** Hold, adopt or reach a third reading. A disagreement that turns on a judgement both readings can defend, with
   no error shown on either side, holds NEEC's verdict and gains the reader's reading as its close call, unless NEEC already
   flags it.
6. **The owner's cells.** CCO-PTF-CIP-SZH's 29 units and the eight owner-decided cells are adjudicated by the same rule; a
   reading that would change one is not applied but put to the owner, and the outside result is published either way.
