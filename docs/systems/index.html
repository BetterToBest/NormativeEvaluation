Systems

NEEC scores 23 economic systems of three kinds: 6 configured national economies, 9 comprehensive systems, 8 mechanisms. The kind decides how a threshold written for a whole population is read. Each system's evidence is in its scoring documents, linked below.

A side-by-side comparison of any three systems is built in. Version 2.0 scores are being finalized; this appears when they are published. Version 1 scores remain on the original NEEC site.
