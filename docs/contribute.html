Contribute

NEEC is built to be challenged. If you think a score, a threshold or a source is wrong, or you have evidence that could change a score, there is a short form for it. Every report is reproduced by the maintainers and answered on the record.

Choose what you want to do

Challenge a score
One criterion for one system: the score you would give, and the evidence for it.
Add evidence
A source, a figure or a new edition that bears on a score, whether or not you dispute the score.
Push back on a threshold
You would set a bar, a reading or a weight differently. Say where and why: disagreements about what ought to count are the ones NEEC most wants recorded.
Report an AI check
You ran the prompt on the Replicate page. Paste the answer, whether it agrees or not.
Propose a system
An economic system NEEC does not score yet, with its founding literature and any implementations.
Propose a criterion
Something an economy ought to achieve that no criterion measures, with the evidence of need and a threshold that could be measured.

The forms need a free GitHub account. The contributing guide explains each route in full, and NEEC_CONTRIBUTING.md gives the method for replication audits and for scoring a new system.

What happens to a report

  1. A maintainer reproduces it: the figure, the source, the reading.
  2. A correction of fact is made. A disagreement of judgment is weighed against the protocol and recorded as a decision with its reason, whichever way it goes.
  3. A score that changes does so through the recorded rescoring pass, applied by a script and checked by the harness, never by hand, and the record links the report.

Reports that confirm a score are logged too. Engage with the evidence and the reasoning rather than with people; those are the reports that change things.
