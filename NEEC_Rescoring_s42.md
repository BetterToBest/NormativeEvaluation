## 3. Decisions and readings (under the delegation)

**3.1 Clause 2: "no differential treatment".** (1) *Interpretations.* The clause could mean (a) that non-participants
bear no burden participants do not bear and lose nothing beyond what participation itself provides; (b) that
non-participants are treated identically in every respect, the benefits of participation included; or (c) that the
system treats no group of people differently. (2) *Preponderance.* The Measurement line names the quantity as
"Penalties for non-participation (differential treatment of non-participants)", which fixes the comparison as one
between participants and non-participants and excludes (c), the subject of other criteria. Reading (b) would fail
every system that provides anything to those who take part, a test that cannot be passed, the mirror image of the
test that cannot fail which part (b1)'s reading 3.1 rejects. (3) *Score* against (a). The clause is shown where the
entry's sources (for a design) or its record establish how non-participants stand, and nothing in them imposes a
burden beyond the benefits forgone. A mechanism whose rules bind only consenting members and levy nothing on anyone
else shows it by its own definition (Mutual Credit); a design whose texts state that non-participants keep its income
floor and the ordinary markets shows it (CCO-PTF-CIP-SZH, Libertarian Minarchism); a design that releases goods only
against its own credits, and aims to replace the market it grows alongside, must say what a non-participant then
receives, and Integral's sources do not. A model that cannot represent a burden on non-participants cannot show the
clause (part (b1), reading 3.1): the design's published model tracks non-participants, but it has no funding side, so
its check that non-participant poverty does not worsen when CCO is enabled cannot fail, and CCO-PTF-CIP-SZH's unit
rests on the design's legislative text instead. (4) *Left open,* since no verdict turns on either: who counts as a
non-participant under a mechanism that applies to everyone in its jurisdiction (a land tax; tax-funded services that
anyone may decline to use), and whether exclusion under a residence- or citizenship-based boundary rule is
differential treatment (Ostrom). Report v2.0 decides both with the four clauses left not estimated.

**3.2 Clause 3: "geographic mobility maintained".** (1) *Interpretations.* The clause could mean (a) that the system
places no restriction on movement, or (b) that it places none and the essential benefits it provides survive a move
within the area it serves. (2) *Preponderance.* The Measurement line reads "Geographic mobility (freedom to relocate
without losing essential benefits)", which adds (b)'s condition; (a) would clear every system that does not fence its
people in. (3) *Score* against (b): the benefits are portable, or available at the destination to the mover on the
same terms; for a system of local or federated units, its design recognises a member's standing across units. A move
beyond the area the system covers is an exit, clause 1's subject. (4) *Applied:* CCO-PTF-CIP-SZH as audited (the basic
unit is national under the draft Act, and Acre Equity moves between housing properties without loss); Integral
cleared (its credit system recognises contribution in one node for access in another at federation scale); Mutual
Credit not shown (a balance and credit line are held in one node); Ostrom short (withdrawal rights belong to a bounded
membership that a newcomer does not acquire by moving in).

**3.3 Clause 4: "voluntary association protected".** Read with the Requirement ("communities can organize around
shared values") and the Measurement line ("Community formation capacity (ability to organize preference-based
associations)"): the entry protects people's freedom to form, join and leave associations, communities organised
around shared values among them. For a design, the protection is a right, rule or institution its sources specify. A
goal the economy should meet is not a protection, and a protection supplied by the host jurisdiction's law is not
credited to an entry whose sources do not specify it, since crediting it would credit the host economy (protocol 3.2;
D29(c)); for a mechanism, the reach rule applies (D28(c)). *Applied:* CCO-PTF-CIP-SZH cleared (its draft
constitutional amendment lets communities organise voluntary associations; its draft Act defines Creator Collectives
as voluntary associations); Doughnut Economics not shown (political voice is a floor of its social foundation, and it
operates through the host's institutions); Ostrom not shown (design principle 7 describes forbearance by external
governmental authorities, which the commons depends on rather than supplies). Libertarian Minarchism, Integral and
Mutual Credit are carried as audited.

**3.4 D31: the five out-of-reach codes are confirmed.** The extension test asks whether the mechanism's own literature
places the quantity inside its design. For Georgism, George's "association in equality" (Progress and Poverty, Book
X, chapter 3) names social cooperation as the engine of progress, not a protection of voluntary associations. For
Universal Basic Services, the legal and democracy sector of the seven-sector framing supplies legal aid, access to
courts and local involvement in the design of services: access to justice, not a protection of association. For
Sovereign Wealth Fund Statism and Islamic finance, neither the entries' documents nor the designs they score place
such a protection, or for Islamic finance geographic mobility, in the mechanism; the partnership contract in Islamic
finance's scored set creates a commercial partnership, not a protection of the freedom to associate. No source was
located for any of the four, so the codes stand and the units are 0.5. The audit found Sovereign Wealth Fund Statism's
C2.5 the one unit whose verdict turns on how an out-of-reach clause counts; D28(c) decides it, so it is not flagged,
as part (b1)'s reading 3.7 did not flag MMT + Job Guarantee's C2.4.

**3.5 CCO-PTF-CIP-SZH, clause 1: the design's own exit terms.** The rationale says "enabling opt-out without penalty",
coded A. The design's own sources set the share of a Public Trust Housing resident's Acre Equity that can be realised
on leaving below face value for five years. The BLEI Foundation Paper's Table 1a gives it as 10-20% of face value in
months 0-6, 30-50% in months 7-24, 60-75% in months 25-60 and 80-90% thereafter, and attributes the discount to
settlement lags, exit fees and a minimal secondary market; the housing paper's 20-year illustration gives $168,000 to
$336,000 of Acre Equity with a cash equivalent of $67,000 to $134,000 upon exit; and the published model applies an
accessible share rising linearly from 0.15 in the first year to 0.85 at five years. (1) *Interpretations.* The
discount could be read as a liquidity feature of an illiquid asset, not a penalty, or as a material cost of exit.
(2) *Preponderance.* The table names exit fees among its causes; the clause asks for exit within three months without
material penalty, and full value takes five years; and the anchor's 0.5 band places even "real economic costs ...
not imposed by the system's own design" below a pass. (3) *Score:* clause 1 is short for the residents of one of the
design's five components (a 20% uptake in the model's reference settings), a part of the population the design serves
(protocol 2.1). Clauses 2, 3 and 4 are cleared, so the unit falls on this clause alone. (4) *Reopening:* exit terms
under which a resident can realise Acre Equity within three months without material loss would reopen the unit, which
would then stand. Whether to change them is the design's choice, not the scorer's.

**3.6 Libertarian Minarchism stands, flagged.** Its one silent clause, the second, is cleared on its founding source:
the minimal state protects everyone in its territory, which Nozick derives by requiring the dominant protective agency
to extend protection to the independents whose private enforcement it prohibits, and the framework for utopia lets
people join communities by consent while no community may impose its vision on anyone who stays out. Its other three
clauses are carried as audited: the threshold's "exit ... without material penalty" and the Measurement line's "time
and resources required to leave system" concern the terms on which a system lets people leave, and the design imposes
none. *The flag* (protocol 5.3): C2.5's own 0.5 anchor example, Status Quo Market Capitalism, and a peer of the same
archetype, Stakeholder Capitalism (0.5), count real economic constraints on exit and mobility against the criterion.
Read that way, clauses 1 and 3 are not cleared for an entry whose own corpus text records survival "entirely
contingent on market participation" with no safety net (its C2.2), and the unit is 0.5. That is a calibration
difference with a peer, not a contradiction in the entry's own sources, so it is a flag rather than an exception. With
the unit standing, CCO-PTF-CIP-SZH no longer dominates Libertarian Minarchism, which joins the frontier.

**3.7 C2.5's anchor.** Its 1.0 example, CCO-PTF-CIP-SZH, moves to 0.5, the eighth anchor example the pass moves. One
corpus 1.0 is left, Libertarian Minarchism. *Decision:* when the pass is applied, the band cites Libertarian
Minarchism as its example with its flag noted, since `build_criteria.py` checks every example against a published
score and one exists. *Alternative rejected:* dropping the example, which part (b4)'s reading 3.5 reserves for bands
with no corpus unit at their value.
