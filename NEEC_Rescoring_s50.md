**3.2 C5.2's scope: "each shown feasible by precedent or component evidence".** (1) *Interpretations.* The scope could
ask (a) that a pathway cite a precedent for its timing (the New Deal's three years, say) or a model of its outcomes;
(b) that each of the five elements be shown feasible by a deployment that took place — of the system itself, or of
institutions of the same kind (a precedent), or, element by element, of the components the pathway deploys, at the
pace and scale it sets (component evidence); or (c) that the pathway be shown to succeed, politically and
economically. (2) *Preponderance:* (b). v2.0 restated C5.2 because the Session 44 clause credited a written plan (review
R4; record, section 4, "plans become feasibility"), and the requirement now says "shown feasible rather than only
planned". "Each" distributes the test over the five elements. Reading (a) is a timeline analogy: that a programme of
another kind was deployed in three years does not show that this pathway's components can be, and a model of the
system's outcomes models no deployment. Reading (c) would score C5.1 (validated mechanisms) and C5.4 (political
support) again, which v2.0 keeps as separate criteria; here political support is taken as given and the question is
operational. (3) *Score* against (b). A configured national economy's precedent is its own history; a comprehensive
system's or a mechanism's is a deployment of its components, which the pass searches for as it did in earlier groups.
A component the entry names as defining, of which no deployment at the pace or scale is located, leaves the element
not shown. A phase count or a date in a plan, a simulation, or a programme of another kind cited for its speed shows
nothing here. (4) *The audit's codes.* v2.0 places all five clauses under the scope, so every A code on C5.2 is re-read
against it, as part (b8)'s reading 3.2 re-read C4.3's; a phrase is cleared where it names, or the pass locates, a
deployment that took place. *Alternative recorded:* reading (a) would change no part (b) unit, since the three that
fall do so on the rapid clause, which none of them states, and Islamic Finance's falls on a clause its sources leave
silent; it would keep CCO-PTF-CIP-SZH's re-read unit at 1.0 (reading 3.4).
