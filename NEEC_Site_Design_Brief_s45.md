# NEEC v2.0 site: design brief

**Session 45, 2026-09-23. Decision 45.5 (the owner's direction):** the v2.0 site has its own visual identity,
unique, elegant and seamless, not the Research Hub's; the Hub links to it, and it links back. This brief governs
stage 6 (`build_site.py`, the site in `/docs`). It sets direction, not pixels; the building session may refine
any value here with a stated reason.

## Subject and reader

The site shows how economic systems compare on one fixed set of criteria, with every score traceable to evidence.
Its readers are researchers, policy readers and curious non-specialists who come to see a comparison, check one
score, or replicate the method. The page earns trust by showing its evidence and its limits, not by decoration.

## The one memorable thing: the score field

Every system is a row of marks, one per criterion, grouped by domain with space between groups: a filled disc for
1.0, a half-filled disc for 0.5, an open ring for 0.0 (a structural failure). Shape carries the score, so the
field reads in greyscale and for colour-blind readers; colour only reinforces it. Rows are ordered by tier, and
tiers are separated by space and a quiet label, not boxes. Focusing or hovering a mark shows the criterion, its
clause verdicts and a link to the evidence; focusing a criterion lights its column across all systems. The field
opens the landing page, with a one-sentence statement of what NEEC is above it. Everything else stays quiet.

Type: Source Serif 4 for prose (18 px, line-height 1.6, measure under 70 characters) and IBM Plex Mono for
criterion codes, figures and the field's labels, with tabular numerals. Both are SIL Open Font License; self-host
them in `/docs` so the site makes no third-party requests. Sentence case throughout: no all-caps labels, no
eyebrow text above headings, no single accented word in a headline.

## Layout

A left-aligned reading column; the score field and the criterion tables break out to the full width.

```
NEEC   Findings  Systems  Criteria  Method  Replicate
One sentence: what NEEC compares, and how.
[ score field: 23 rows x 28 marks, grouped by domain and tier ]
Findings, stated with their reasons and limits (the no-1.0 finding; provisional totals)
Report · Paper (PDF) · Replication kit
```

Pages: a system page (its row enlarged, domain subtotals shown with their addends, the register of contestable
calls); a criterion page (the Pass Threshold's clauses, the named indicators, which systems clear each clause);
Method (the protocol in plain words, tiers, weighting schemes); Replicate (the kit, the harness, how to check one
score); Visual Suite v3 as a no-build page.

## Motion and voice

One orchestrated moment: on first load the field's marks settle domain by domain, in under 600 ms, and not at all
under `prefers-reduced-motion`. Copy is plain and active; findings are stated with their limits; the author's
design among the systems is disclosed on the landing page, as the README does.

## Build and delivery

Static HTML, CSS and vanilla JavaScript, generated by a committed script from `neec_corpus.json`, `criteria.json`
and `neec_scores.csv`; a harness check asserts that the site's data equals the corpus. No build tools, no
frameworks. WCAG 2.2 AA: every mark keyboard-reachable, and the field is a real table whose cells carry the score
as text for screen readers. A print stylesheet for criterion pages. GitHub does not start a Pages build for
commits pushed with a workflow's token, so `land-session.yml` requests one after each landing; the owner enables
Pages once (Settings, Pages, deploy from branch `main`, folder `/docs`). A footer link returns to the Research Hub.

Checked against common generated-design defaults: the ground is cool, not cream; no near-black with an acid
accent; structure comes from spacing, not hairline rules or identical cards.
