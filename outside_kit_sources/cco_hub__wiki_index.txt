 System Interaction Model

 How the five framework architectures depend on, govern, and reinforce one another — the structural logic of Compassionism as a unified system.

 Each framework occupies a distinct functional layer. Understanding their relationships is essential for implementation sequencing — dependencies must be in place before dependent systems can operate effectively. The table below formalizes these relationships for research and policy analysis.

 Economic Layer

 CCO

 Creative Currency Octaves — generates value signals and circulation incentives through the dual-currency system

 Asset Layer

 PTF

 Public Trust Foundations — builds collective ownership and community wealth through community-operated businesses

 Asset Layer

 PTH

 Public Trust Housing — creates collective housing equity through resident-owned community properties

 Spatial Layer

 SZH

 Social Zone Harmonization — organizes physical and social structure through coordinated community planning

 Democratic Layer

 CIP

 Citizens Internet Portal — governs currency operations and democratic participation across all systems

 Framework Dependencies & Relationships

 Relationship 
 Description 

 PTF depends_on CCO 
 PTF accepts basic units as payment; CCO funds PTF operations and provides the currency infrastructure PTF businesses require 

 PTH depends_on CCO 
 Housing costs settled in basic units; CCO provides the economic security that makes collective housing participation viable 

 CIP governs CCO 
 CIP manages conversion rates, merit assessment, and basic unit distribution — the democratic layer controls the economic engine 

 CIP governs PTF + PTH 
 Democratic oversight of all collective assets; residents and members vote on key decisions through the CIP platform 

 SZH stabilizes PTF + PTH 
 Zoning shapes service and housing deployment; SZH ensures PTF businesses and PTH communities are spatially coherent 

 CCO feeds_into SZH 
 Creative Collectives anchor zone formation; preference and economic activity patterns inform how social zones are structured and evolve 

 PTF enhances CCO 
 PTF venues reduce Creative Collective overhead by 40–60%; PTF workers receive a conversion bonus, and essential-market incentives benefit CCO participants
