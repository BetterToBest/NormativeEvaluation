Implementation Roadmap - Better To Best Research Hub 

 Overview

 This roadmap outlines the systematic deployment of CCO-PTH-CIP-SZH systems across four phases, from initial pilots to full-scale integration. Each phase builds upon previous successes while expanding scope and complexity.

 Establish proof-of-concept implementations in controlled environments to validate core systems and gather operational data.

 Validate quality assessment mechanisms
