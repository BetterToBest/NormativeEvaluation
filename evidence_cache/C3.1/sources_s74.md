- United States, 2020: the first official data showing the shock (initial claims for the week ending 21 March,
  released 26 March 2020; `fred_claims_weekly.csv`); the CARES Act, signed 27 March (`cares_act_wikipedia_lead.txt`);
  the first Economic Impact Payments deposited from 10 to 15 April and the first Federal Pandemic Unemployment
  Compensation paid by the states from the first half of April (IRS and US Department of Labor releases).
- Singapore, 2020: the Unity (18 February), Resilience (26 March), Solidarity (6 April) and Fortitude (26 May) Budgets,
  each passed by Parliament, and the Solidarity Payment of S$600 credited from 14 April (Ministry of Finance); real
  GDP fell 3.9% in 2020 (Department of Statistics).
- Qatar, 2020: the Emir's directive of 15 March 2020, a QR 75 billion package for the private sector (loan
  guarantees, deferrals, fee and utility exemptions), and the Workers' Support and Insurance Fund's payments of unpaid
  wages (Qatar News Agency; Ministry of Labour).
- China, 2020: the State Council's measures from February to May 2020 (the extension of unemployment insurance and
  temporary living allowances to uninsured and migrant workers, the expansion of dibao); real GDP grew 2.2% in 2020
  after a 6.8% year-on-year fall in the first quarter (National Bureau of Statistics).

  Economics 24: the relief record of the 1932-33 and 1946-47 famines.

  Journal of Economic Behavior and Organization 72: WIR turnover counter-cyclical.
