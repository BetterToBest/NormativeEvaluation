# C4.5 Exploitation Elimination: sources the blind re-read cites but does not cache (Session 71)

Prose, not digest-checked. Each was read on 2026-10-18 through a web search or the publisher's page; the figure the
reading uses is quoted in `blind_s71.py`, where it is held. The cached files beside this note are listed and checked
by `evidence_cache_s71.py`.

- **United States, employment-based health insurance:** US Census Bureau, *Health Insurance Coverage in the United
  States: 2025* (P60-291): 53.5% of people held employment-based coverage in 2025 (53.8% in 2024, P60-288).
- **United States, voluntary quits and unemployment insurance:** US Department of Labor, *Comparison of State
  Unemployment Insurance Laws*, chapter on nonmonetary eligibility (oui.doleta.gov/unemploy/pdf/nonmonetary.htm):
  every state disqualifies a claimant who leaves work voluntarily without good cause. The 2024 and 2025 PDFs were not
  at the paths tried (HTTP 404); the chapter's HTML page was located by search.
- **Stakeholder capitalism's record:** Bebchuk and Tallarita, "The Illusory Promise of Stakeholder Governance",
  *Cornell Law Review* 106 (2020), on the 2019 Business Roundtable statement's signatories (cited, not cached).
- **China, unemployment insurance:** Ministry of Human Resources and Social Security, *2024 Statistical Bulletin on
  Human Resources and Social Security*, as reported (the ministry's own page was not located): 245.89 million insured
  at the end of 2024, against about 734 million employed (National Bureau of Statistics, 2024).
- **Singapore, public housing:** Department of Statistics Singapore, resident households by type of dwelling (Latest
  News & Data, 2025): 77.2% of resident households live in HDB flats.
- **Singapore, Work Permit holders changing employer:** Ministry of Manpower, "Hiring an existing manufacturing or
  services worker" and "Hiring existing worker in construction sector" (mom.gov.sg): a transfer needs the current
  employer's consent, except in a window before the permit expires; TWC2 (twc2.org.sg, 2021) on the window's
  narrowing for construction workers.
- **Qatar, the 2020 labour reforms:** Law No. 17 of 2020 (minimum wage: QAR 1,000 basic, plus QAR 500 housing and QAR
  300 food where the employer does not provide them) and the 2020 decree-laws removing the No Objection Certificate
  for changing employer (ILO, "Dismantling the kafala system and introducing a minimum wage mark new era for Qatar's
  labour market"; secondary sources number the decree-laws 18 and 19 of 2020 differently). ILO, *Labour reforms in the
  State of Qatar: Coming together around a shared vision* (2022), as reported by Al Jazeera (1 November 2022): 348,450
  job changes from November 2020 to August 2022, against about 18,000 in 2019; workers still face obstacles to
  leaving a job, retaliation by employers among them.
- **Qatar, population:** non-Qataris are about 88% of residents (Planning and Statistics Authority estimates as
  commonly reported; not cached).
