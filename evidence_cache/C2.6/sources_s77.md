# C2.6: sources the Session 77 blind readings of the six configured economies cite (prose; not digest-checked)

Cached by `evidence_cache_s77.py`: `owid_fh_civil_liberties_score_s77.csv` (Freedom House's civil-liberties score,
0-60, 2015-2025, the eight economies); `owid_fh_civil_liberties_rating_su_s77.csv` (its 1-7 rating, the USSR and Russia,
1972-1991); `owid_vdem_civil_liberties_s77.csv` (V-Dem's Civil Liberties Index, 1960-2025; V-Dem codes the Soviet Union
as Russia); `fh_fiw2026_cl_f1_extract.txt` (Freedom in the World 2026's country pages: the score header and question
F1); `us_law_c26_extract.txt`, `dk_law_c26_extract.txt`, `fi_law_c26_extract.txt`, `se_law_c26_extract.txt`,
`no_law_c26_extract.txt`, `su_law_c26_extract.txt`, `cn_law_c26_extract.txt`, `sg_law_c26_extract.txt` (the law in
force, each passage headed by its source, its digest as served and its line). Session 70's `us_cfr_42_70.14.html` and
`us_cfr_42_71.37.html` (federal quarantine and isolation orders) are read with the United States extract.

Not cached, cited, or not reached:

- Singapore, consent: no statute, code or constitutional provision was located; consent to treatment rests on the
  common law of negligence (the Court of Appeal's *Hii Chii Kok v Ooi Peng Jin* [2017] SGCA 38, on the duty to
  advise), not fetched.
- Qatar: almeezan.qa, the State's legal portal, serves its statutes as a client-rendered application whose data
  the container could not reach (every page returns the same 6 KB shell), so neither the consent provisions of the
  law on the practice of medicine nor the communicable-diseases law (Decree-Law No. 17 of 1990, as amended in 2020)
  was read.
- Norway, compulsion: lovdata.no refused the container (HTTP 405), so the Communicable Diseases Control Act
  (smittevernloven, 1994) and the Mental Health Care Act were not read; the Directorate of Health's annotated text of
  the Patient and User Rights Act, section 4-1, names them as the exceptions to consent.
- Denmark's Psychiatry Act and Sweden's Compulsory Psychiatric Care Act (LPT, 1991:1128) are the exceptions to
  consent in those economies; the Danish act's periodic review and court route are cached, the Swedish act was
  fetched (data.riksdagen.se) but not extracted, no clause turning on it.
- The window's emergency measures (V-Dem's Pandemic Violations of Democratic Standards Index; the Oxford COVID-19
  Government Response Tracker) were not read: every economy's clause 3 is short on the law in force, or (Denmark,
  within a configuration short elsewhere) does not decide the clause.
