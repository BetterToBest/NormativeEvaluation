Not cached, cited:

- Session 70's finding stands for the European Social Survey fallback: the ESS fields only European countries, so
  clause 3 has no named measure for the United States, China, Singapore or Qatar.
- Finland's citizens' initiative (kansalaisaloite, constitution section 53, since 2012): by March 2023, 64 initiatives
  had reached Parliament and five had become law, two more being adopted in modified form (figures by search,
  2026-10-18, from the Eduskunta's and Participedia's summaries; not a primary count). Sweden has no national
  citizens' initiative with a right to a decision; Denmark's borgerforslag (2018) and Norway's municipal
  innbyggerforslag are not read, since Finland's and Sweden's records decide the clause for the configuration.
- The United States: no national process of citizen-initiated proposals with a right to a decision exists; state-level
  initiatives exist in about half the states.
- Singapore's and Qatar's voting-age turnout counts residents who are not citizens (the protocol's population rule,
  section 3.2): Singapore's 2025 general election, 92.8% of registered voters and 51.0% of the voting-age population.
- China: the Constitution's preamble and article 1 (the leadership of the Communist Party); direct elections only to
  county and township people's congresses, under the Electoral Law; the National People's Congress chosen indirectly.
  The Party's membership was 99.18 million at the end of 2023 (its Organisation Department's statistical bulletin,
  June 2024), under a tenth of China's roughly 1.1 billion adults.
- Centrally Planned Socialism: the 1977 Soviet Constitution, article 6 (the Party as "the leading and guiding force");
  single-candidate elections to the soviets.
