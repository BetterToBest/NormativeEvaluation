# C4.6: sources the Session 77 blind readings of the six configured economies cite (prose; not digest-checked)

Cached by `evidence_cache_s77.py`: `oecd_exp_morsc_gbd2019_s77.csv` (the OECD's Mortality, morbidity and welfare cost
from exposure to environment-related risks, built on the GBD 2019: deaths per million inhabitants, both sexes, all
ages, attributed to ambient particulate matter, household air pollution from solid fuels, unsafe water source, unsafe
sanitation and no access to handwashing, 1990-2019, the eight economies; crude rates, not age-standardized);
`who_gho_sdgpoison_s77.csv` (the WHO's SDG 3.9.3 unintentional-poisoning rate, both sexes, 2000-2023, the eight
economies; Session 70's `SDGPOISON.json` held four); `us_law_c46_extract.txt` (the PREP Act's immunity, the Covered
Countermeasure Process Fund, CERCLA's liability); `nordic_law_c46_extract.txt` (Denmark's Environmental Damage
Compensation Act, Sweden's Environmental Code chapter 32, Finland's Environmental Damage Compensation Act);
`cn_law_c46_extract.txt` (the Civil Code's articles 1229, 1234 and 1235); `su_environment_c46_extract.txt`
(Wikipedia's article on the Soviet Union's environmental issues, whose text is the Library of Congress Country Study's;
the cached sentence on enforcement carries "citation needed" tags). Session 65's `wjp_rol_sf62_2012_2025.csv` (the
World Justice Project's sub-factor 6.2, at the repository's root) is read for clause 3. Session 70's WHO files
(`SDGAIRBOD.json`, `air42.json`, `SDGWSHBOD.json`) confirmed the SDG series' single years; this session's queries of
the GHO's other air-pollution codes (SDGAIRBODA, AIR_50, AIR_52) found 2021 alone or nothing.

Not cached, cited, or not reached:

- The GBD's own results tool (IHME) needs registration, and Our World in Data's GBD charts are marked
  non-redistributable (HTTP 403 on download), so the OECD's GBD 2019 dataset, which ends in 2019 and is not
  age-standardized, is the series read.
- Claims decided and paid: no claim decided against those who caused the harm, and paid, within the window was
  located in a cached source for any economy. For the United States the clause is short on the PREP Act whatever the
  record; known US cases (e.g. *Exxon Shipping Co. v. Baker*, 554 U.S. 471 (2008), punitive damages paid in 2009) are
  not cached.
- Norway's Pollution Control Act (chapter 8, liability for pollution damage) and the Nordic group-action statutes
  (Denmark's Administration of Justice Act, chapter 23a; Sweden's Group Proceedings Act, 2002:599; Finland's Class
  Action Act, 444/2007; Norway's Dispute Act, chapter 35) were not read: lovdata.no refused the container, and no
  reading turned on the others once no claim record was named.
- Singapore's and Qatar's liability law was not read (Qatar: almeezan.qa, see C2.6/sources_s77.md); both are short on
  Freedom House's F1, whatever the statutes provide.
