# C3.4 Epistemic Adaptability: sources the blind re-read cites but does not cache (Session 71)

Prose, not digest-checked. The figure each reading uses is quoted in `blind_s71.py`, where it is held. The cached
files beside this note are listed and checked by `evidence_cache_s71.py`.

- **United States, clause 4:** Curry and Shibut, "The Cost of the Savings and Loan Crisis: Truth and Consequences",
  *FDIC Banking Review* 13(2), 2000 (the crisis after the Depository Institutions Deregulation and Monetary Control
  Act of 1980 and the Garn-St Germain Act of 1982; about $124 billion to taxpayers); Financial Crisis Inquiry
  Commission, *Final Report* (January 2011), conclusions (widespread failures in financial regulation and
  supervision; decades of deregulation).
- **United States, clause 1:** the federal funds rate (cached series) and the Tax Cuts and Jobs Act of 2017 (Public
  Law 115-97), which cut the corporate rate from 35% to 21%.
- **United States, clause 3:** the Administrative Procedure Act, 5 U.S.C. 706 (a reviewing court holds unlawful and
  sets aside agency action found arbitrary, capricious or otherwise not in accordance with law).
- **United States, clause 2:** the cached CPI-U and federal funds series (CPI-U year on year above 4% from April
  2021; the first rate rise in March 2022); the CARES Act signed 27 March 2020 (as group 2.9 recorded for C3.1).
- **China:** Xiao and others, "Excess All-Cause Mortality in China After Ending the Zero COVID Policy", *JAMA Network
  Open* 6(8), 2023 (about 1.87 million excess deaths among people aged 30 and over, December 2022 to January 2023);
  the end of zero-COVID announced on 7 December 2022; the "three red lines" for property developers' borrowing
  (August 2020) and the developer defaults that followed (Evergrande, December 2021); the one-child policy relaxed to
  two children (2016) and three (2021); the Administrative Litigation Law (1989, amended 2014).
- **Qatar:** the November 2024 constitutional referendum returning the Shura Council to appointment; the 2020 labour
  laws and the ILO's 2022 job-change figures (evidence_cache/C4.5/sources_s71.md).
- **Centrally Planned Socialism:** the VASKhNIL session of August 1948 and Lysenko's removal in 1964-65; the June 1962
  Soviet price rise on meat and butter and the Novocherkassk shootings; state retail prices of staples held until
  the April 1991 price reform; Poland's food price rises of December 1970, June 1976 and July 1980, each met by
  strikes and the first two reversed; the Law on State Enterprise (1987) and the Law on Cooperation (1988) before
  the collapse of 1989-91; Hungary's New Economic Mechanism (1968). Standard histories; none cached.
- **Fully Automated Luxury Communism:** Bastani, *Fully Automated Luxury Communism: A Manifesto* (Verso, 2019), a
  commercially published book (evidence_cache/designs_index_s70.json); the entry's own scoring records its governance
  as largely unspecified (Report v1.6, FALC C2.4 and C4.4).
