NEEC compares economic systems against one fixed set of normative criteria, with the evidence behind every mark.

Normative means the criteria say what an economy ought to achieve: security from poverty, freedom to choose, resilience in a crisis, fairness across groups and generations, and a credible way to get there. Each of the {{n_systems}} systems is scored 1, 0.5 or 0 on each of the {{n_criteria}} criteria. A 0 is a structural failure, and failures, not totals, decide a system's tier.
