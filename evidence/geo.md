> **STRUCTURAL FAILURE.** This criterion asks whether participants can build a $70,000+/20-year household buffer through the system's own mechanism. NEEC's own UBI evaluation (System 7) establishes the governing precedent: UBI scores a structural failure here even with a full poverty-elimination Pass, because income consumed for living expenses is not an asset-accumulation vehicle regardless of size, unless specifically structured as one. The same reasoning that fails UBI's wealth-building criterion applies to a bare Citizen's Dividend with equal force.
