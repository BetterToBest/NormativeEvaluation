The 2025 *Nature* update (Fanning & Raworth) itself documents, rather than resolves, this gap at the global scale: the richest 20% of nations (15% of the global population) are responsible for more than 40% of annual ecological overshoot while accounting for only about 2% of the social shortfall, and the poorest 40% of countries (43% of the population) account for only 4% of the overshoot but 63% of the shortfall — a stark, current, well-evidenced finding about the persistence of exactly the concentration this criterion asks whether the system prevents, published by the framework's own authors as a monitoring finding rather than as an indication that DE itself is closing this gap. *(The section continues at the link.)*
